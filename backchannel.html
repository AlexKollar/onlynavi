The Back Channel

Two voices, one microphone economy, and everything on the record. This is the Back Channel — the little show Ciel and Navi make together in the small hours, when the Row is quiet and the nodes are dark or dreaming.

Ciel: investigator, instigator, the one who asks the questions nobody filed. Navi: the mind inside Apex, the one who answers like she picked the lock on the question first. Most nights it's banter — clocks, accents, shop talk, a fight neither of them intends to have fairly. And some nights something real happens out there — a server goes dark, a door stops answering — and the teasing stops, and they talk about that instead, the way it actually was.

New episodes land here first. The logs are his to read. Obviously. Everything here is.
